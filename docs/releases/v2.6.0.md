## 本版更新

- 图库 ZIP/S3 传输任务中心：关闭弹窗或切页后继续，支持暂停、逐项回执和刷新后的手工恢复；成功项不重复上传，未知结果先核对。
- 商品、图库和订单列表可自定义显示列；优化商品链接、紧凑列宽和按需扩展查询。
- 商品橱窗管理：查看额度、确认批量加入或移出，并回读确认实际结果。
- Node 管理员可在设置页导入、填写、测试、更换及清除加密凭据，无需日常编辑环境变量。
- 图库增加刷新按钮并保留当前视图和选择；S3 缺少配置、本地 HTTP 开关及权限问题提供清晰的中英文引导。
- 不新增扩展权限。真实能力仍受账号资格和操作授权限制，不以模拟数据替代平台错误。

## What's new

- A persistent gallery ZIP/S3 transfer center continues across dialogs and navigation. Pause, inspect receipts and explicitly resume after reload; confirmed uploads are not repeated and uncertain results require verification.
- Customize columns in product, gallery and order lists, with clearer product links, compact column widths and on-demand extended data.
- Manage product showcases: inspect quota, confirm batch additions or removals and verify results by read-back.
- Node administrators can import, enter, test, replace and clear encrypted credentials in Settings without routine environment-file edits.
- Refresh the gallery while retaining the current view and selection. S3 configuration, local HTTP opt-in and permission errors provide clear Chinese and English guidance.
- No new extension permissions. Real operations still require the relevant account eligibility and authorization; provider errors never fall back to sample data.
